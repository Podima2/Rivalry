## Design Context

### Users
Runners racing friends across cities or being matched with verified strangers. They use Rivalry outdoors before, during, and after a run, often on a phone in bright daylight.

### Brand Personality
Confident, athletic, and welcoming. The interface should make a remote race feel tangible and social while staying clear and calm enough to use outdoors.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode first, with warm paper-like neutrals, a vivid vermilion accent, and editorial typography. Avoid crypto styling, financial dashboard patterns, social-feed clutter, and aggressive betting or esports visuals.

### Design Principles
1. Make race distance, readiness, and progress easy to read at a glance.
2. Design for one-handed use, bright daylight, and large touch targets.
3. Use color to focus attention on race actions and meaningful state changes.
4. Keep wallet and chain mechanics invisible in the user experience.
5. Show location only when the race mode and privacy rules allow it.
